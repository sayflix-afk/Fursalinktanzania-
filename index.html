Stay safe while searching

FursaLink points you to sources, but always verify an opportunity before sharing information or applying.

1. VerifyCheck the official organisation website and closing date.
2. Protect yourselfDo not share passwords, PINs, one-time codes or sensitive identity documents with strangers.
3. Avoid upfront feesBe suspicious of anyone demanding money in exchange for a guaranteed job or scholarship.
Transparency: FursaLink Tanzania is an independent resource directory, not a recruiter, employer or scholarship provider. The vacancy summaries on this page are reviewed manually, not an automatic live feed. We do not guarantee employment, scholarships or income.
